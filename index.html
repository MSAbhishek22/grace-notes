04

How grounding works

  1. Bundle, don't browse. 43 public-domain KJV passages, chosen across ten pastoral topics, ship with the page. corpus.js
  2. Retrieve, don't generate. Your words are matched against each passage's keywords, topics and text — in your browser, with no model and no server. retrieval.js
  3. Cite every claim. The answer lists each passage's gist beside its reference, then quotes it word for word. app.js
  4. Say “not found” honestly. Below a grounding threshold, the answer is a plain “not found in the provided passages” — never invented doctrine. retrieval.js

Faithfulness & Responsible AI — the hackathon's own criterion — is the point of this design: a small, checkable corpus a pastor can audit beats a large model a congregation cannot verify. A human stays in the loop: every answer is meant to be read, checked against the cited verse, and weighed by a church leader, not obeyed blindly.
