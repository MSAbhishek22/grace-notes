## Suggested submission description (for the platform form)
> Grace Notes is a grounded church Q&A companion: ask a faith or pastoral
> question and it answers only from 43 bundled King James Version passages,
> with a citation for every claim — and says "not found in the provided
> passages" instead of inventing doctrine. It also offers a verse & topic
> explorer and sermon-note grounding that turns your notes into grounded
> follow-up questions. No backend, no model, no data leaves the device:
> the whole corpus and retrieval run in the browser, so a pastor can audit
> everything it can ever say. Built solo, free, in the hackathon window.
